0–60, quarter mile and braking, timed by your iPhone

Launch Timer combines the iPhone's GPS with its motion sensors, 100 readings a second, to time launches, rolling pulls and stops — and shows a live dashboard on your car's screen.

Timing a run

  1. Mount the phone firmly. Any angle works, but it must not move during the run.
  2. Stop, and wait for Ready.
  3. Launch. The clock starts on the first movement and stops when you lift.
  4. For braking, get above 60 mph (100 km/h) and brake to a full stop.

Only time runs on a closed course or drag strip.

Car screen

Open hakob17.github.io/launchtimer/car in the car's browser (Tesla or any car with a web browser), and enter the pairing code from the app's Car screen tab.

Accuracy

On a flat, straight road with a clear view of the sky and a firmly mounted phone, times are typically within a tenth of a second. Tunnels, tall buildings, a loose mount and slopes make them less accurate; the app shows the slope of each run.

The 1-ft rollout setting starts the clock after the car has rolled one foot, as drag strips and most magazines do. It makes times about 0.2–0.3 s quicker.
